# Method sources

This file is **identical in both skills**. Change both copies together.

The process (four due-diligence checks, a team decision, then
commercialization through MVP, customer validation, go-to-market and
operational readiness) is the team's own, from its *Idea Evaluation and
Commercialization Template*. These are the published methods it borrows
from:

| Use | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Investment and launch reviews (gates) | [Stage-Gate](https://www.stage-gate.com/about/stage-gate-innovation-performance-framework/discovery-to-launch-process/) |
| First-market selection, sizing, pricing, acquisition | [MIT Disciplined Entrepreneurship](https://entrepreneurship.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/delta-v-impact-study.pdf#page=34) |
| Customer discovery | [NSF I-Corps](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps/cohorts), [YC on customer interviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z1iF1c8w5Lg) |
| Market checks and economics | [SBA market research](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis), [SBA break-even](https://bec.www.sba.gov/breakevenpointcalculator) |
| Business model, tests, evidence, pricing | [Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas), [Test Card](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/validate-your-ideas-with-the-test-card), [evidence strength](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/how-strong-is-your-innovation-evidence), [pricing experiments](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/how-to-run-pricing-experiments) |
| MVP, positioning, operational readiness | [Eric Ries on MVPs](https://leanstartup.co/resources/articles/what-is-an-mvp/), [April Dunford on positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/books), [AWS Operational Readiness Review](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/ops_ready_to_support_const_orr.html) (adapt for digital services) |

**Interaction patterns** are borrowed from
[VCV8's public workflow](https://vcv8.com/how-it-works):

- an idea in one paragraph plus optional materials;
- three review lenses;
- a short decision brief first;
- follow-up and "what if" questions;
- revisions when evidence changes.

VCV8's proprietary scoring, benchmark calculations and data access are
**not** used. Evidence strength stays qualitative, with reasons.

The template's own links point to the same sources: NSF I-Corps, the YC
guide to talking to users, the MIT Disciplined Entrepreneurship roadmap, SBA
market research, Stage-Gate Discovery-to-Launch, the Strategyzer guide to
testing a business model, the SBA break-even calculator, the Business Model
Canvas, the Test Card, evidence strength, pricing experiments, Eric Ries on
the MVP, April Dunford on positioning, and the AWS Operational Readiness
Review.
